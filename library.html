Theories, models, and frameworks

Common theories, models, and frameworks in social work and the social sciences. Each entry gives its type, what it says, its seminal source, whether it was substantially revised and by whom, its common propositions, and its key concepts.

How to use it: Choose one that explains why your outcome happens. Then read the seminal source. The propositions here are paraphrased; your paper needs the exact wording and page number from the source. If it was revised, decide which version you are using and cite that one.
