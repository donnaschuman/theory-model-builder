Social Theory Model Builder

D. L. Schuman, The University of Texas at Arlington

Turn a theory, a population, and an outcome into one clear research question, testable hypotheses, and an editable path figure, with coaching at each step.

Who it's for

Doctoral and master's students and early researchers in social work and related fields.

What you leave with

  • A plain-language research question
  • Numbered hypotheses
  • A path figure (SVG / PNG / Mermaid)
  • A summary of your decisions

How it works

  1. Who & outcome
  2. Theory
  3. Causes, including social determinants of health
  4. In-between & moderators
  5. Figure & hypotheses
