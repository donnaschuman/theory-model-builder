Social Theory Model Builder

D. L. Schuman, The University of Texas at Arlington

A step-by-step tool for building a conceptual model from a theory. You enter a population, an outcome, a theory, and the theory's propositions. The app produces a path figure, a research question, and hypotheses.

Steps

  1. QuestionPopulation, outcome, and how the outcome is measured
  2. TheoryThe theory, its seminal source, and its key propositions
  3. ModelCauses, mediator, and moderators
  4. ConceptsA definition and source for each concept
  5. ArrowsDirection, line style, and reason for each arrow
  6. ResultsFigure, research question, hypotheses, and concepts table

For doctoral and master's students in social work and related fields.

About

The app runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your work is saved on this device; use Save my work to keep a copy or move it to another computer. Anonymous visit counts are collected with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal information, none of your work). Use the app as a planning aid and check theory constructs against your course readings.

AI disclosure

D. L. Schuman designed the tool and its teaching content. The code, interface text, and tests were drafted and revised with Grok (xAI) and Claude (Anthropic; Claude Opus 5.5). The author reviewed and tested the result and is responsible for it. The app does not use AI. It does not generate propositions, definitions, reasons, or hypotheses; it assembles them from what you enter. Sentence starters are fixed templates.
